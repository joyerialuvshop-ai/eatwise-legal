EatWise Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EatWise ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the EatWise mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

Profile & Health Data

The App allows you to enter profile information such as age, height, weight, goals, meal logs, water intake, and weight entries. This data is primarily stored locally on your device.

Food Photos

If you choose to log meals by photo, images may be processed using AI services to estimate nutritional information. Photo logging is optional.

Device & Usage Data

We may collect limited device and diagnostic information such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash reports, performance data, and interaction events to improve the reliability and performance of the App.

Account & Cloud Backup Data

If you choose to connect your Google or Apple account, EatWise may store your profile information, nutrition logs, hydration logs, workout logs, and related app data securely using cloud services such as Supabase in order to provide backup, restore, and cross-device synchronization features.

Connecting an account is optional. If you do not connect an account, most data remains stored locally on your device only.

2. Advertising & Tracking

The App may display advertisements, including banner and native ads.

We may use advertising partners such as Google AdMob. These partners may collect or receive device identifiers (such as Advertising ID on Android or IDFA on iOS) and other technical information to provide and measure advertisements.

On iOS devices, we request permission for tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing the device advertising identifier for personalized ads.

If you decline tracking or consent where applicable, non-personalized ads may still be shown.

For users in certain regions (including the EEA and UK), we may request consent for personalized advertising through a consent management platform (e.g., Google UMP). If consent is not granted, only non-personalized ads will be served where possible.

Optional Meta Ad Measurement on iOS

Some iOS versions of EatWise may offer optional measurement of Facebook and Instagram advertising using the Meta SDK. This feature is off by default and is not available in every version. It is separate from Google AdMob advertising and from EatWise's own service analytics.

Where this feature is offered, EatWise enables it only after you separately choose to allow Meta measurement, confirm that you are at least 18, and grant iOS App Tracking Transparency permission. If any of these requirements is not met, the integration stays off. Your age confirmation and measurement choice are stored on your device; EatWise does not send your date of birth or age confirmation to Meta through this integration.

If enabled, Meta receives installation and app-opening/session activity together with device and technical SDK information. This can include advertising or app-scoped device identifiers, IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, language, and event/session timing.

We use this integration to attribute installations and understand the effectiveness of our Facebook and Instagram ads. Meta may associate the information with other information it holds, including your Meta account, and process it under Meta's Privacy Policy . This measurement is not described as anonymous.

EatWise does not use your meals, food photos, nutrition entries, weight, goals, HealthKit or fitness data, scan/barcode contents, or subscription and purchase-verification information for this Meta measurement. Our integration does not supply Meta with your EatWise account ID, email address, or other account profile fields. Automatic purchase-event logging is disabled.

This does not change the processing needed for other EatWise features described elsewhere in this policy.

You can decline without changing app features, scan limits, subscription prices, or access. In versions that offer this feature, you can change your choice in Profile → Meta ad measurement, or withdraw iOS tracking permission in your device settings.

Turning Meta measurement off does not turn off Google AdMob or EatWise's separate service analytics; their processing and available choices are described separately.

Withdrawing stops future app-initiated Meta measurement and turns off advertising-identifier collection by this integration. An SDK that has already initialized cannot be unloaded immediately: requests already started may finish, and SDK-internal configuration activity may continue during the current app session.

Close and relaunch the app after withdrawing to keep the SDK inactive. Withdrawing does not delete data already sent to Meta. SDK state or events recorded while permission was valid may remain on the device and may be delivered if you later opt in again.

EatWise stores your measurement preference locally to remember and respect your choice until that local app data is removed or replaced. Meta's retention and privacy-rights practices for data it receives are explained in its Privacy Policy; turning this feature off is not a request to delete Meta-held information.

For requests about information held by EatWise, contact eatwise.support@gmail.com .

3. Subscription and Purchase Information

Apple or Google processes your payment details. EatWise does not receive or store your full payment card number, security code, or store-account password.

To verify purchases and provide Premium access, we may process the store, product identifier, transaction or original transaction identifier, a protected installation or app-account identifier, purchase status, purchase and expiration times, renewal status, and store-signed purchase proof. Google Play purchase tokens are stored only as one-way hashes in our transaction records.

We use this information to verify purchases, restore access, prevent fraud and duplicate transactions, apply subscription changes such as renewal, cancellation, expiration, refund, or revocation, and provide subscription support.

Purchase proof is sent securely to Apple or Google for verification and processed through our Supabase backend. We do not use subscription verification information for advertising or sell it.

We retain transaction and entitlement records as needed to provide access, handle restores and disputes, prevent fraud, meet legal and accounting obligations, and protect the service.

Deleting app data or an EatWise account does not automatically cancel a store subscription. Manage or cancel the subscription separately through your App Store or Google Play account.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to support certain features of the App, including:

  • Google Sign-In authentication
  • Apple Sign-In authentication
  • Supabase cloud storage and authentication services
  • Advertising services such as Google AdMob
  • Meta SDK for optional iOS installation and app-activation advertising measurement, where offered and permitted as described above
  • Crash reporting and diagnostics services such as Sentry
  • AI-based food analysis services
  • Apple Health / HealthKit integrations, if enabled by the user
  • Apple App Store and Google Play purchase verification services

These services may process limited technical data necessary to provide their functionality.

5. Data Storage & Retention

Most user profile and nutrition tracking data is stored locally on your device by default.

If you connect a cloud account, certain app data including profile information, meal logs, hydration logs, weight entries, workout logs, and profile photos may also be securely stored using cloud infrastructure to support backup and restore functionality.

You may disconnect your account at any time from within the App. Disconnecting an account removes the connection from the current device but may not automatically delete previously backed-up cloud data.

Some features that rely on third-party services may process limited data externally to provide functionality such as advertisements, crash reporting, AI food analysis, authentication, or cloud synchronization.

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the App's functionality.
